Help and support

Drag to aim, lift your finger, then tap briefly to swing. Ending a drag or holding your finger down does not swing.

Use Settings → Career backup and transfer to export or import progress. Keep a separate backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

For help, email the app version, device, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords or payment details.
